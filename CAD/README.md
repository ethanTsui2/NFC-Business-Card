# CAD

This folder is for the final card CAD and manufacturing geometry.

## Final Design Specifications

- 85.6 x 54 mm overall size
- approximately 3 mm corner radius
- 0.8 mm overall thickness
- internal NFC cavity
- face-down front graphics
- QR code on the back

The earlier 1.6 mm design is superseded. Check the actual NFC tag thickness, cavity depth, and remaining PLA above and below the tag in the 0.8 mm geometry. Do not reuse the thicker card's pause height without checking the sliced model. The saved November 24, 2026 project contains three 0.8 mm bodies. The negative-volume cavities have bounding dimensions of 26 × 26 × 0.10 mm (two bodies) and 26 × 25 × 0.12 mm (one body). These are disc-shaped/elliptical negative volumes, not rectangular pockets. Their nominal Z ranges above the card base are 0.35–0.45 mm and 0.34–0.46 mm respectively. Confirm the physical tag fits and inspect the sliced cavity before printing.

This BOM separates items I bought specifically for this card from equipment and consumables I already had.

## Cost Notes

The NFC order was a 50-pack of 25 x 25 mm InnoHHustle NTAG215 tags, so the full purchase cost is not the cost of one finished card.

At TTD 62.90 for 50 tags:

- NFC purchase cost per tag = **TTD 1.258**
- one card uses **1 NFC tag**
- unused tags can be used for later cards or other projects

The holographic build plate and 0.2 mm hotend are reusable tooling, so I do not treat their full purchase price as the material cost of a single card.

PLA usage and purge waste depend on the final slicer profile and are not included here yet. Once the final print is locked, I can calculate the approximate material cost per card from Bambu Studio's filament usage.
